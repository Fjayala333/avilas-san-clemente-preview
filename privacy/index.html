Website information

Privacy

Last updated September 29, 2026.

This website is designed to help guests view menus, learn about Avila’s El Ranchito, find locations and connect with third-party services for directions, ordering and social media.

Information this site uses

The website does not include an account, checkout or contact form. When measurement tools are enabled, they may collect standard technical information such as browser type, approximate location, visited pages and interactions with menu, call, directions or order links. This information is used to understand and improve the guest experience.

External services

Links to online ordering, maps, Instagram and other location websites open services operated by third parties. Their privacy practices apply after you leave this website. Online orders and payments are handled by the linked ordering provider, not by this website.

Your choices

You can adjust cookie and privacy settings in your browser, and you can contact the restaurant by phone if you prefer not to use an external online service.
