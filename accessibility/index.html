Accessibility

Avila’s El Ranchito San Clemente wants every guest to be able to explore this website, understand the menu and plan a visit comfortably.

Our accessibility goal

We are working toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA as our technical target. Accessibility is an ongoing effort that includes automated checks, code review and manual testing. This statement is not a certification or a guarantee of legal compliance.

What we are doing

Our work includes keyboard-accessible navigation and dialogs, visible focus styles, descriptive image text, accessible HTML menu and location information, scalable layouts, reduced-motion support, and labels and states for interactive controls.

Need help or another format?

If any part of the site is difficult to use, or if you need menu or location information in another format, call us at (949) 498-5000. Please tell us which page or feature caused difficulty so we can assist you and improve the experience.

Third-party services

Online ordering, maps and optional Instagram content are operated by third parties. Their accessibility is partly outside our control. If a third-party service creates a barrier, call us and we will help with restaurant information or ordering options available by phone.

Restaurant access

For current information about restaurant access, seating or specific accommodations, call before your visit so the team can assist you directly.

Last reviewed: September 30, 2026. This page and the site’s accessibility should be reviewed as the website and third-party services change.
